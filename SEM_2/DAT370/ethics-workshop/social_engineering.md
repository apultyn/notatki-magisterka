### Cognitive Pathway
![[Pasted image 20260925092244.png]]

Working memory - didn't use it, just worked with what was given to her
Decision-making - based on the information she percieved as true the decision was right - grandchild in trouble -> help

### Congnitive Factors
- short term - definitely vigilance, there was no questioning that the caller might not be her grandson, or that he is really in trouble, may be a little stress because she is probably not comfortable with modern technology
- long term - the most important factor is age, because with age comes decreasing mental sharpness and worse hearing, also the personality thing, family responsibility of grandma loving her grandchild and wanting to protect it
