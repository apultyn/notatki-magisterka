### Psychological safety 
- team members fell safe to take risks and be vulnerable, believe that they are taken seriously
### Equal speaking time
- 4 rules
- rule of contributing - every team member must contribute to the issues
- rule of inclusion - you have to include the other team members in the process
- rule of connecting - each team member has a responsibility to connect to the contributions of others (e.g asking if you didn't understand something)
- rule of integrating - you have to make conclusions and integrate what has been said by others
## Tips from lecturer
- meeting regularly 
- having clear communication channels
- having respect for each other and group processes
- talk through the "how" as well as the "what"
- **team agreement** - nice tool to clarify expectation and set preliminary team norms
## 5 stages of team development
- forming - teammates are strangers, team establishes ground rules
- storming
- norming
- performing
- adjourning
## Final advice
- establish communication channels
- clarify expectations and discuss other commitments
- decide when your revisit your way of working rules
- choosing a way of tracking progress (easy to use!)
- start working quickly 
- fika - sit together and work together even if on different things (helps communication)
